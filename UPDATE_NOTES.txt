================================================================
  UPDATE NOTES — Owners, Coaching & Engine Overhaul
================================================================

A large update spanning new systems, a deep simulation audit, and
a wide pass of bug fixes and polish.


NEW: OWNER SYSTEM
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Owners set an expectation tier (rebuild → contend →
    championship) based on market size, roster, and facilities,
    with an expected-win target.
  • Your GM contract carries season-long sub-tasks (All-Star
    marks, development, cap management) that feed a satisfaction
    score.
  • At season's end the owner weighs wins vs. expected wins,
    sub-task completion, prior-season history, and patience, then
    decides to extend or move on.
  • New Owner tab consolidates all of this, with a guided
    walkthrough.
  • Owner welcome conversation when you sign a new GM contract.


COACHING
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Reworked coaching skills and scheme effectiveness so coach
    fit affects results.
  • Coach Actions overhauled and surfaced more clearly in the UI.
  • Free-agent coaches are auto-hired when you sim the offseason.


AI & TRADING
------------------------------------------------------------
  • AI teams evaluate their own direction (contend vs. rebuild)
    and act on it — shopping veterans, pursuing stars, and making
    larger trades rather than filler deals.
  • AI-to-AI trades reshape the league over time.
  • User-to-AI trading is open during the offseason (locked during
    the live draft for now).
  • Fixed a timezone bug that allowed AI trade offers in the
    opening week; AI now holds off until Week 2.


SIMULATION & LOGIC
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Full audit of systems affecting game outcomes: simulation,
    substitutions, and ratings.
  • Every player attribute and badge is now actually read by the
    sim, enforced by an automated audit that fails the build on
    drift.
  • Playoffs fully play out and crown a champion before any owner
    firing, retirements, or offseason decisions run.
  • Rebuilt season-end → offseason sequencing (awards →
    retirements → contract decision) so popups no longer stack or
    race.
  • Tuned playoff payouts, award eligibility (aligned the
    All-Defensive / All-League games thresholds), and player
    motivation.


FACILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Retuned starting facility levels across the league.
  • Only the user's team degrades over time; this now correctly
    follows you if you switch teams (the old team reverts to AI
    handling).


POLISH & FIXES
------------------------------------------------------------
  • Corrected team names and records throughout (fixed the
    duplicated-city "Brooklyn Brooklyn" display).
  • Refreshed campaign UI, added GM-level badges, rookie history
    in player cards, and recovery-report popups.
  • Fixed a light-mode display bug.
  • Removed a redundant API pull on every home-page navigation.


------------------------------------------------------------
Thanks for playing. Feedback continues to shape each release.
