===============================================================================
AUDIT: HOW AI TEAMS MANAGE COACHES & COACH SETTINGS
===============================================================================

Two questions audited:
  (A) Coach LIFECYCLE  — do AI teams extend/replace coaches based on results?
  (B) Coach SETTINGS   — do AI coaches adapt schemes/rest per game & context?

VERDICT
  (A) CONFIRMED STATIC. AI coaches are assigned once at campaign start and are
      never re-evaluated, extended, or replaced — it's frozen "by design"
      (there's an explicit comment saying so).
  (B) MOSTLY STATIC. Schemes DO refresh once per offseason to track roster
      changes, but they are chosen on roster-fit ALONE — no opponent, no
      standings, no season-timing. There is NO per-game gameplan, and rest is
      driven only by raw fatigue, never by "we've clinched, rest the stars."


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART A — COACH LIFECYCLE (static, confirmed)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

COACH DATA MODEL — frontend/src/engine/data/coaches.js, engine/data/coachBadges.js
  - Attributes: offensiveIQ, defensiveIQ, playerDevelopment, strictness,
    gameManagement; plus overallRating and a tier ('free' | 'good' | 'really_good').
  - Badges (coachBadges.js): player_whisperer (development bonus), offensive_/
    defensive_mastermind, late_game_genius, players_coach.
  - Development IS modeled: the playerDevelopment attribute + player_whisperer
    badge grant a development multiplier consumed by the evolution system. So
    "a rebuilder targeting a development coach" is implementable with existing data.
  - Contract: coach.contractYearsRemaining exists on the schema.

ASSIGNMENT AT CAMPAIGN START — CampaignManager.js generateCoach() (~2745)
  - Each team's coach tier comes from MODE_TO_COACH_TIER (contender=1 ... rebuilder=4),
    i.e. tied to the team's preset campaignMode, not to performance.
  - overallRating sampled from COACH_TIER_RANGES[tier]; contractYearsRemaining =
    randInt(1,4) — set once, at generation.

OFFSEASON HANDLING — CampaignManager.js (~2448)
  Explicit comment + code:
    "AI team coach contracts are NOT decremented (frozen by design; only the
     user's coach uses the hire/fire system)."
  - ONLY the user's coach contract is decremented and can expire -> forces a
    hire. AI coach contractYearsRemaining is never touched.
  - hire/fire/resign exist ONLY in the user UI (TeamManagementView.vue,
    HireCoachModal.vue, stores/team.js). No AI service hires/fires/extends.
  - A free-agent coach POOL exists and refills each offseason
    (topUpCoachPool), but AI teams never draw from it.

CONSEQUENCE
  - A rebuilder that wins 60 games keeps its tier-4 coach. A contender that
    collapses keeps its tier-1 coach. Coach quality is frozen at day 1 and
    never reflects results or a shifted team direction.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART B — COACH SETTINGS / SCHEMES (roster-fit only; no game context)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SCHEME MODEL — engine/simulation/CoachingEngine.js
  - Offensive schemes: balanced, motion, iso_heavy, post_centric, three_point,
    run_and_gun (drive pace, transition rate, play-type weights).
  - Defensive schemes: man, zone_2_3, zone_3_2, zone_1_3_1, press, trap.
  - Substitution strategy: staggered, platoon, tight_rotation, deep_bench
    (drive per-player minute caps).

HOW AI SETTINGS ARE CHOSEN — CampaignManager.js (~1241 at create, ~2510 each offseason)
  - team.coaching_scheme = selectBestCoachingScheme(teamPlayers, team.coach, prev)
  - AI teams ALWAYS get a fresh scheme each offseason, so trades/draft DO shift
    their style year-over-year (this is the nuance: not 100% static).
  - BUT selection (engine/coaching/CoachStrategyService.js _pickOffensive/_pickDefensive)
    keys off ROSTER ATTRIBUTES ONLY (team speed, 3pt count, post scorer, etc.).
    Zero inputs for opponent, standings, seed security, or season timing.

HOW SETTINGS ARE USED IN A GAME — GameSimulator.js (~478)
  - initializeGameFromData() loads home/away offensive+defensive+sub schemes
    from the persisted team object ONCE, then they are fixed for the whole game.
  - AI has NO per-game scheme re-selection and NO in-game adjustment. The only
    mid-game scheme change path, applyAdjustments() (~2439), is USER-ONLY.

THE ONLY CONTEXT ADAPTATION THAT EXISTS (player layer, not gameplan)
  - Fatigue-based minutes: computeAITargetMinutes (SubstitutionEngine.js ~1057)
    trims minutes ~15% for fatigue >= 50 and benches restMode players (0 min);
    skipped entirely in the playoffs.
  - restMode is set by PlayerEvolution.js (~339) HYSTERETICALLY off raw fatigue
    (on at a high fatigue threshold, off when fatigue <= 15). Honored by AI.

WHAT DOES NOT EXIST (the substance of the concern)
  - No per-game gameplan: AI never evaluates its offensive/defensive settings
    before a game against the actual opponent or its own available healthy players.
  - No matchup awareness (e.g., switch to press vs a slow half-court team).
  - No standings/clinch awareness: there is NO logic where a team locked into a
    seed strategically rests fatigued (but not exhausted) stars down the stretch
    and restores them for the playoffs. The only "clinch" code is a news headline
    (BreakingNewsService) and playoff-series clinching (PlayoffManager) — neither
    affects regular-season rotations. Rest is purely fatigue-reactive.

SCENARIO CHECK (the 72-3, seed-locked example)
  - Today: those stars only sit if their raw fatigue crosses the restMode
    threshold; the engine does not proactively rest them because the seed is
    secure, and does nothing special to ramp them back up for round 1.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPROVEMENT DIRECTIONS (for discussion — not yet planned)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. AI COACH LIFECYCLE (offseason)
   - Decrement AI coach contracts; at expiry (and/or after a bad year vs
     expectations), evaluate extend-vs-replace using last 1-2 seasons' record
     against the team's expectation (its mode/direction).
   - Let AI teams hire from the existing free-agent coach pool (topUpCoachPool),
     preferring fit-for-direction: rebuilders weight playerDevelopment /
     player_whisperer; contenders weight gameManagement / late_game_genius /
     scheme mastermind.

B. CONTEXT-AWARE COACH SETTINGS
   - Pre-game gameplan: let AI (re)select scheme + rotation depth using opponent
     profile, its own available healthy players, and game importance, instead of
     a single season-long setting.
   - Standings/seed-aware rest: when a team is mathematically/near-locked into a
     seed late in the season, proactively rest fatigued-but-not-exhausted stars
     and ramp minutes back for the playoffs (extend the existing restMode/
     targetMinutes machinery with a "seed security" input).

Existing pieces to reuse: selectBestCoachingScheme / CoachStrategyService,
computeAITargetMinutes + restMode (SubstitutionEngine/PlayerEvolution),
generateCoach + topUpCoachPool + coach tiers/badges, analyzeTeamDirection
(AITradeService) for a team's competitive window, and standings in seasonData.
