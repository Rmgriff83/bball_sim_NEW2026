TRADE SYSTEM — FULL EXAMINATION

1. ARCHITECTURE: THE TWO DIRECTIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

USER -> AI  (user builds an offer, AI accepts/rejects)
  TradeCenter.vue wizard
    -> tradeStore.proposeTrade()
    -> evaluateTrade()  (pure value verdict)
    -> if accepted, tradeStore.executeTrade()
    -> TradeExecutor.executeTrade() moves players/picks, writes history, fires news.

AI -> USER  (AI generates offers into your inbox)
  Once per game day, tradeStore.fetchPendingProposals()
    -> generateWeeklyProposals() rolls each AI team through:
         gate -> need -> target -> build offer -> self-verify
    -> saves <=1 new proposal/day to seasonData.tradeProposals.
  You then accept (acceptProposal), reject (rejectProposal -> starts cooldowns),
  or negotiate (prefills the wizard as a counter-offer).

AI <-> AI  (background league activity)
  processAiToAiTrades() from game.js, up to 3/week, bilateral accept required.

The single brain for all three is evaluateTrade() — everything funnels through
the same receiving-vs-giving value comparison.


2. THE RULES & WEIGHTS (AI -> USER OFFERS)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Gates before any offer is generated (generateWeeklyProposals):
  - Before the Feb 5 trade deadline only.
  - Opening week (first 7 days from Oct 21): silent.
  - One pending proposal per team at a time.
  - Cooldowns: rejected/expired team -> 30 days; any rejected player -> 14 days
    blocks ALL teams from targeting them; exact team+player -> 30 days.
  - Base probability 1.2%/team/day (~1-2 offers/week across 29 teams).
    Deadline month: x2 for contenders/win-now, x1.5 others.
  - Proposals expire after 3 days.

Who the AI will target on your roster (isPlayerAvailableForTrade + retention filter):
  - Rating < 82 -> always targetable.
  - Rating >= 82 -> only if on your trading block, morale < 50, 1 year left,
    or retention < 35.
  - Then a second filter: retention <= 60. Net effect — the AI essentially
    never pursues a happy, signed 82+ star.

Difficulty weights (DIFFICULTY_CONFIGS) — acceptance generosity:

  Difficulty     | threshold | fairness_mult | star_protection | pick_sensitivity
  ---------------+-----------+---------------+-----------------+-----------------
  rookie         |   0.25    |     1.6       |      0.8        |     0.85
  pro            |   0.15    |     1.0       |      1.0        |     1.0
  all_star       |   0.10    |     0.7       |      1.25       |     1.15
  hall_of_fame   |   0.05    |     0.45      |      1.5        |     1.30

  Acceptance rule:
    receiving >= giving - max(giving x threshold x fairness, 1)
  On rookie you can underpay ~40%; on HoF you must give ~98% fair value.

Direction archetype (analyzeTeamDirection, blends record + roster, record weight
grows over the season) drives DIRECTION_MULTIPLIERS — e.g. contenders pay a 1.25x
premium for incoming stars and discount picks to 0.65x; rebuilders value youth
1.25x and picks 1.4x.

Per-player value stack (calculateReceivingValue / calculateGivingValue):
  base tradeValue
    x young-player premium
    x contract bargain/overpaid multiplier
    x timeline fit
    x direction multipliers
    x positional-need 1.15x
    x retention-risk discount
    + expiring-contract bonus for rebuilders.


3. BUGS & ISSUES FOUND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### HIGH SEVERITY

1. Draft picks are valued at a flat 5 everywhere.
   getPickValueFn defaults to () => 5 in AITradeService and is NEVER passed by
   any caller (confirmed: zero call sites). Picks carry real trade_value data
   (used in the UI), but the evaluation engine never reads it.
   Consequences in BOTH directions:
     - You can request a team's franchise pick and it counts as ~5 value — a
       lopsided-trade exploit.
     - The AI can't value picks, so it rarely manages to offer them and badly
       undervalues picks you include.

2. Cap-aware validateSalaryCap is imported but never called.
   The only salary enforcement is a client-side check in TradeCenter.vue, which:
     - Ignores capMode entirely — always applies the 125% rule, so easy mode's
       "no cap" promise is broken and hard-cap mode is never enforced anywhere.
     - Is completely bypassed on the acceptProposal path (AI offers aren't
       cap-checked).
     - Skips the check when outgoingSalary === 0 (line 116) — you can acquire a
       max-salary player for picks-only with no salary matching.

3. The trade deadline doesn't block user-initiated trades.
   generateWeeklyProposals and processAiToAiTrades both gate on isBeforeDeadline,
   but proposeTrade / executeTrade / acceptProposal have NO deadline check
   (confirmed: no deadline references anywhere in the trade store or trade UI).
   The game posts a "no more trades can be made this season" news item while the
   user can keep trading freely.

### MEDIUM SEVERITY

4. recalculatePayrolls is never called.
   After a trade, players get new teamId but team total_payroll is never updated.
   That value feeds the Cap Space display (TradesTab.vue) and fetchTradeableTeams'
   cap_space, so both drift after any trade until recomputed elsewhere.

5. Asymmetric-trade risk on stale rosters.
   Picks have a robust find-anywhere lookup that throws if missing — but players
   don't. In movePlayer, a player who left the AI roster (e.g. an AI<->AI trade
   between propose and execute) is silently skipped while the other side still
   moves -> a one-sided trade. There's no re-validation at execution that the
   requested assets still belong to the counterparty.

6. Displayed team direction != evaluated direction.
   fetchTradeableTeams computes a separate simple winPct heuristic (only
   win_now/ascending/rebuilding — never title_contender) for the team-picker UI,
   while evaluateTrade uses the 4-archetype blend. The "interest"/direction you
   see can contradict how the AI actually values the deal.

7. Trading-block type coercion gap.
   isPlayerAvailableForTrade uses raw tradingBlock.includes(player.id), without
   the String() coercion the store applies everywhere else (explicitly "to
   prevent number vs string from IndexedDB"). A type mismatch would leave a
   blocked player unprotected from AI targeting.

### LOW / DESIGN NOTES

8.  AI never chases your happy stars (gate #2 above) — realistic, but can feel
    like the AI ignores your best players.
9.  getPlayerStatsFn isn't passed in proposeTrade, so contract value uses
    rating-only expected salary (no production adjustment) for user-initiated deals.
10. evaluateTrade models salary only as a bargain/overpaid multiplier, never as
    cap matching — AI "fairness" is pure talent value.
11. Minor: redundant receivedName/receivedList recompute in processAiToAiTrades.

user-side deadline) are the ones materially distorting trade outcomes and
exploitable by the player.
