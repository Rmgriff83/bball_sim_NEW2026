0. THE QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do AI teams make smart, direction-aware roster decisions throughout the WHOLE
campaign -- e.g. a rebuilder accumulating first-round picks (thinking years
ahead), a contender spending picks/youth to add a star for a playoff push -- or
are they passive? The trigger: taking over a non-playoff team that had only 4
rostered players.


1. TL;DR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The decision FRAMEWORK is real and direction-aware. There IS a team-direction
  model, direction-specific trade-value multipliers, projected draft-pick
  valuation, and direction-weighted drafting. It is smarter than "random."

* But the EXECUTION is thin in exactly the ways you noticed:
  1. No "big-swing" trades. A contender cannot bundle multiple picks + youth for
     one star. Trades are small, value-matched, capped at ~1-2 picks + 1
     secondary player.
  2. The trade market is quiet and regular-season-only. ~8% chance per team-pair
     on weekly Monday ticks, max 3 trades/week, hard stop at the Feb 5 deadline,
     and ZERO offseason / draft-day trades. Rebuilders never get to "stockpile
     picks" at scale.
  3. Rebuilders are reactive, not proactive. They value picks highly and give up
     vets cheaply IF a contender comes calling, but never proactively shop their
     veterans.
  4. Every AI roster decision is expectation/prestige-BLIND. Direction is
     inferred from record + roster only -- never the owner's mandate or the
     franchise's facility tier.
  5. Roster maintenance can fail. The roster-refill is deferred and has a quality
     floor that, against a depleted free-agent pool, can leave AI teams below a
     full roster -- the direct cause of the 4-player team you inherited.


2. HOW AI TEAMS DECIDE TODAY (THE MACHINERY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2a. Team direction model -- analyzeTeamDirection()
    frontend/src/engine/ai/AITradeService.js:328-399. Produces one of
    title_contender | win_now | ascending | rebuilding by blending:
    - Record (winPct from standings) -- weight ramps with games played, capped
      at 0.7  (recordWeight = min(0.7, gamesPlayed/82 * 0.9)).
    - Roster (star power: #players >=85 OVR =1, >=82 =0.5; youth score; avg
      overall; core age).
    - Hard overrides: mathematically eliminated (>=20 games, winsNeeded >
      gamesRemaining) -> rebuilding; starPower >=0.8 & winPct >=0.65 & >=15 games
      -> title_contender.
    - Does NOT read: playoff seeding, owner expectation, facility tier, prestige.

2b. Trade value perception -- direction multipliers
    AITradeService.js:60-101  DIRECTION_MULTIPLIERS. Each direction perceives
    assets differently. Representative values:
    - rebuilding:    pickReceiveDiscount 1.4 (wants picks), vetGiveEase 0.75
      (sheds vets), youngGiveEase 1.2 (hoards youth), starGiveProtection 0.85
      (willing to move stars).
    - title_contender: starReceivePremium 1.25, starGiveProtection 1.35 (guards
      stars), pickReceiveDiscount 0.65 (picks matter little), pickGiveSensitivity
      0.75 (will spend picks).
    calculateReceivingValue / calculateGivingValue (lines ~645-766) apply these
    plus young-player premium, contract value, expiring bonus, timeline fit,
    positional need. evaluateTrade (lines ~783-835) accepts when
    receiving - giving >= -fairnessThreshold. Direction-specific rejection copy
    at lines ~587-631 ("We're looking to acquire draft picks in any deal", etc.).
    VERDICT: the value model genuinely encodes rebuild vs. contend preferences.

2c. Draft-pick valuation -- PickValuationService.js
    computePickValue (lines ~215-238): slot base value (SLOT_ANCHORS #1~=22 ...
    #30~=1.8, round-2 cliff), x draft-class-strength multiplier, x
    future-uncertainty discount (yearsOut: 1y=0.9, 2y=0.82, 3y=0.74, 4y+=0.65).
    Future picks regress toward league mean (25%/yr, cap 80%) since the owner's
    future record is unknown, and projectPickSlot ranks by the ORIGINAL team's
    record -- so a rebuilder's pick is correctly worth more (lottery odds).
    VERDICT: pick valuation is sound -- but future picks are discounted, which
    (with the trade caps below) suppresses multi-year pick stockpiling.

2d. Draft selection -- frontend/src/services/AIDraftService.js
    selectRookieDraftPick (lines ~183-284): talent base dominates (direction sets
    potential-vs-overall blend: rebuilding 70/30 ceiling, contender 30/70 floor,
    win_now 40/60, ascending 55/45), + small upside/youth/work-ethic/badge-
    synergy bonuses, + positional need scaled by pick slot (negligible top 5,
    dominant by round 2), + ±1.5 jitter, then weighted-random among top 3.
    VERDICT: drafting is direction-aware best-player-available -- solid.

2e. Offseason contract decisions -- AIContractService.js
    Cuts (processTeamCuts), re-signings (evaluateResigning, Bird-rights aware),
    FA signings (processTeamSignings, fill toward TARGET_ROSTER_SIZE = 14), and
    assessDraftCapital (rebuilders with draftRichness > 0.5 cut more
    aggressively). All read direction, draftCapital, capSituation -- none read
    owner expectation/tier. Roster constants: MIN_ROSTER_SIZE 10,
    TARGET_ROSTER_SIZE 14, MINIMUM_SEASON_ROSTER 14.


3. DECISION CADENCE ACROSS ONE CAMPAIGN YEAR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHEN                              DECISION                            DIR-AWARE?
In-season, every Mon (to Feb 5)   AI<->AI trades (processAiToAiTrades) Yes
In-season, ~Feb 5                 Trade deadline -- ALL trading stops  n/a
Season end                        Contract expiry -> FA, retirements   Retire:yes
                                  (enterOffseason CampaignManager:2286-2349)
Offseason step 1                  Cuts only; signings+backfill skipped Yes
                                  (runAIRosterManagement{skip...:true} :2304-2315)
Offseason, 14-day FA window       Re-signs + FA signings (fill to 14)  Yes
                                  (generateAIFreeAgencyOffers, daily)
Offseason, after FA               Rookie draft (all 60 picks)          Yes
                                  (simFullOffseason / draft room)
Offseason, startNewSeason         Backfill AI teams to 14              NO (qual.)
                                  (ensureMinimumRosters CampaignManager:~2721)

TAKEAWAY: roster work is concentrated in the ~2-week offseason, plus a low-volume
weekly in-season trade trickle that hard-stops at Feb 5. There is NO offseason or
draft-day trading at all, so "thinking years ahead" via pick accumulation can't
really happen.


4. THE GAPS (WHERE IT FALLS SHORT OF YOUR BAR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAP 1 -- No big-swing star trades.
  _findAiToAiTrade (AITradeService.js:~1470-1655) only generates: (a) player-for-
  player swap, (b) player-for-picks (contender buys ONE vet from a rebuilder,
  capped ~1-2 picks), (c) player + 1 pick upgrade. There is no "3 firsts + a young
  player for a star" construction. So the marquee win-now move you described
  doesn't exist.

GAP 2 -- Quiet, regular-season-only market.
  AI<->AI: base 8%/pair/Monday, x1.5-2 near the deadline, max 3 trades/week, min
  roster 8 to trade, opening-week quiet, nothing after Feb 5, nothing in the
  offseason. Net: a handful of small trades per season league-wide.

GAP 3 -- Rebuilders are reactive.
  Direction multipliers make a rebuilder ACCEPT a pick-heavy return, but nothing
  makes a rebuilder INITIATE shopping its expiring vets. Pick stockpiling only
  happens if a contender happens to roll a trade against them -- and only 1-2
  picks at a time.

GAP 4 -- Expectation/prestige blindness.
  Confirmed across engine/ai/ and engine/season/: AI contract/trade/draft logic
  never reads owner expectation, facility tier, or prestige. Direction is purely
  record+roster. So a demanding-owner contender and a patient rebuild-owner behave
  identically given the same roster/record, and a franchise's mandate never shapes
  its roster strategy.

GAP 5 -- Roster maintenance can silently fail.
  Backfill is deferred (skipBackfill:true at enterOffseason) to the FA window +
  startNewSeason. ensureMinimumRosters (AIContractService.js:846-912) fills only
  from the free-agent pool and STOPS at a 50-OVR quality floor (minRating default
  50). A team that sheds many aging contracts year over year, against a league-
  wide FA pool that other teams have already picked over, can be left below 14
  with no emergency generation (only the USER-team finalizer generates tryout
  bodies). AI teams thus can persistently shrink.


5. WHY THE TEAM YOU TOOK OVER HAD 4 PLAYERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chain: every team starts with 15 (LeagueRosterGenerator.js). At enterOffseason the
team's expiring contracts are released to FA and retirees removed, cuts run, but
signings and backfill are intentionally skipped. The owner contract-decision /
team-switch happens at THIS offseason-hub moment -- i.e. the single most depleted
point of the year, before the FA window and before startNewSeason's backfill.
switchUserTeam reads the roster as-is with no fill, and crucially
ensureMinimumRosters EXCLUDES the user team -- so the team you adopt is never
auto-filled. A non-playoff team (more expiring/aging deals) lands at the bottom of
that range -- hence 4. It's a timing + coverage gap, amplified by the quality-floor
depletion in Gap 5.


6. RECOMMENDATIONS (FOR DISCUSSION -- NOT YET IMPLEMENTED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ordered by impact-to-effort:

1. Fix takeover/maintenance (bug-level, small): fill a taken-over team (and
   guarantee AI teams) to a viable size at the moment of takeover and at the hub
   -- reuse the existing aiFinishUserTeamSetup (FA pool -> emergency tryout
   fillers) so no one inherits a skeleton; optionally lower/raise the minRating
   floor with emergency generation so AI teams can never sit below the minimum.

2. Make direction expectation-aware (medium): feed owner expectation + facility
   tier into analyzeTeamDirection (or as a bias) so a championship-mandate
   franchise leans win-now and a rebuild-mandate franchise leans long-term,
   independent of a noisy early record.

3. Open the trade market (medium): add offseason/draft-day AI trades and raise
   in-season volume/cadence so pick movement actually happens across the year.

4. Add big-swing patterns (larger): let contenders bundle multiple picks + a young
   player for a star, and let rebuilders proactively shop expiring vets for pick
   packages -- the two archetypal moves you described.
